## Stage 4 — normalize aliases
Map linguistic variants to canonical concepts.

## Stage 5 — association graph
Create typed edges.

## Stage 6 — clustering
Cluster products and human realities separately and jointly.

Use:
- deterministic rules for product/engineering clusters;
- evidence-driven semantic similarity for text;
- graph communities for relationship structure;
- human-readable canonical territories.

## Stage 7 — contradiction detection
Do not only find similarities.
Find tensions and exceptions.

## Stage 8 — distillation
Produce the smallest decision-relevant representation.

## Stage 9 — counterfactual engine
Break assumptions deliberately.

## Stage 10 — concept/testing
Create candidate concepts with explicit lineage and kill criteria.

## AI/LLM role
LLM may:
- suggest candidate labels
- propose aliases
- summarize evidence
- suggest candidate relationships
- generate counterfactual questions

LLM may NOT:
- create product truth
- create health evidence
- create verified relationships
- silently promote candidate labels to truth

Candidate suggestions must pass rules/evidence validation.
